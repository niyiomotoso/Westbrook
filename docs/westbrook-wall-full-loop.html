The complete loop, start to finish

Every step below is one moment on the wall, 8 seconds apart. Read it left to right, then down. Each screen shows the slide its neighbour showed a step earlier, so a slide travels from the right screen to the left. This sample hotel has three room types and every photo uploaded, so the loop is 11 steps and 88 seconds. After step 11 it goes straight back to step 1. A hotel with more rooms or photos has a longer loop, and a missing photo is skipped. A message can cut in at any point, then the loop restarts at step 1. The time card changes city every 6 seconds and the review page shows the next review each step.

Your logo and your rooms

The W on the Welcome page, the name and logo on the left screen and the logo everywhere else is a stand-in. Reception uploads the real WestBrook logo once and it replaces every one. Room types work the same way as photos: add as many as the hotel has, each with a name, a price and a picture. Here the hotel has four, so an Executive room joins the loop after Deluxe. A room type with no picture is left out.

The wall at rest

Two cards sit on top of the photos and stay put: name and greeting on the left, the time on the right. Nothing sits on the middle screen, so the photo and its price get the room.

Reviews have their own page

The review page is one slide in the loop, with no photo. Each time the wall changes, it shows the next review, so three reviews flip one after another. With one review, it stays. With none, the page is skipped. The reviews here are samples, first name only, with the guest's permission.

Tags and what gets skipped

Each photo or video gets one tag when it is added. The tag decides the caption: rooms show a price, the other places show their name, and the restaurant adds its breakfast hours. A tag with no photo is left out of the loop, and so is the review page when there are no reviews.

How the wall moves

Every 8 seconds all three screens change together with a soft cross-fade. Each takes the slide its right-hand neighbour was showing, and a new one enters on the right.

How long things last

Every slide in the loop lasts 8 seconds, so the three screens change together. A video in the loop is trimmed to the best 8 seconds for the same reason. A message is not part of the loop, so its video can run longer.

Messages

A welcome back, birthday or anniversary message shows on all three screens for 12 seconds, or as long as its video if it has one, up to 15 seconds. It has a picture if there is one and is plain if not. Then the loop restarts from the first slide, so nobody misses it.

  • Add a picture or video, or leave it out for the plain version.

Seasons

Switched on by date, then off again by itself. Independence Day is on 1 October. For Christmas, decorations in Lagos go up from late November, with the big public light-ups around the end of the month, and many people take theirs down around 6 January, though Lagos displays often stay until mid-January. Christmas here runs 1 December to 6 January.

Decided

  • The loop starts on the Welcome page, then the rooms.
  • The real hotel logo is uploaded and replaces the stand-in everywhere. Any number of room types can be added, each with a name, a price and a picture.
  • Christmas runs 1 December to 6 January.
  • Independence Day shows on 1 October only.
  • Reviews are typed in by reception, each with a tick that the guest agreed.
  • Breakfast is 7 – 10 am, on the restaurant slide and the Good to know page. Pool, gym, lobby and bar show their name only.
  • Three templates to start: welcome back, birthday and wedding anniversary, all editable. Reception can also write a new message of their own.
  • Videos in the loop last 8 seconds. Videos in messages last up to 15 seconds.
  • Version 1 is photos only. Videos come later, and so does a photo or video running across all three screens.
